# Wattsmart: AI-Powered Smart Energy Optimization System
Wattsmart is an intelligent energy management system designed to tackle the global energy crisis by leveraging AI-driven predictions and personalized recommendations. It helps users optimize their energy consumption, reduce electricity costs, and minimize carbon emissions — contributing to a more sustainable future.

## Features
1. **Real-time Energy Usage Graphs**: Hourly, daily, and weekly trends in line/bar charts, with peak/off-peak hour distinctions.
2. **AI-Predicted Energy Consumption**: Estimates future usage and billing costs.
3. **Live Energy Usage Meter**: Circular gauge for real-time energy tracking and alerts for high consumption.
4. **Personalized Energy Tips**: AI-driven tips like "Shift laundry to off-peak hours to save 20%."
5. **Reports & Analytics**: Compare energy usage over time, with appliance-wise consumption and cost breakdowns.
6. **User Customization**: Add/remove appliances, set preferences, and receive real-time alerts for high consumption.
